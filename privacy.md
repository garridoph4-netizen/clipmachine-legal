# ClipMachine Cortes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

ClipMachine Cortes ("the App") is a personal tool, run locally on the developer's own computer, that creates short vertical video clips from public live streams and uploads them to the developer's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

## Data we access
- **TikTok account (via Login Kit):** basic profile information (display name) and an access token, only for the account of the App's own developer, used to upload videos.
- **Videos:** clips created by the App and uploaded by the account owner.

## How data is used
Data is used only to upload the account owner's clips to TikTok as drafts or posts. We do not sell, share, rent or publish any personal data. The App has no public users other than its developer.

## Storage
Access tokens are stored only on the developer's local computer and can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions > Manage app permissions). Temporary video files are deleted after publication.

## Third parties
Videos are uploaded to TikTok and YouTube under their own terms and privacy policies. Transcripts of public live streams are processed by an AI service to select clips; no TikTok account data is sent to that service.
